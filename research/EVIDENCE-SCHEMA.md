```text

Notes:
```

## Evidence states

**A — Directly documented**  
Explicitly described by a primary technical or provider source.

**B — Experimentally demonstrated**  
Supported by controlled causal evidence.

**C — Independently observed**  
Detected through repeated independent measurement.

**D — Correlational**  
Associated without established causal identification.

**E — Hypothesis**  
Plausible and testable but not demonstrated.

**F — Unknown**  
Evidence is currently insufficient.

## Observability status

Record each major variable as:
- directly measurable,
- inferable,
- inaccessible / unknown.
